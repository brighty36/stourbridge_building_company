FAQs

Before work starts.

How much will it cost?

Condition, structure and finish all matter. We price from a room-by-room scope.

How long will it take?

A room may take weeks; a whole house may take several months.

Do I need planning permission?

Usually not for internal work, but external changes may need permission.

Do Building Regulations apply?

They may apply to structure, insulation, drainage and electrical work.

Can we stay in the house?

Often for small phased jobs. Larger renovations may be easier with the house empty.
