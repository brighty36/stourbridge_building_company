# Photo plan

The draft now uses the temporary photographs in the `images` folder. Their current filenames are deliberately unchanged. Compress and rename them before publication, then replace them with original, permission-cleared project photographs where noted. Shoot landscape images wider than the intended crop, avoid visible house numbers and personal documents, and get homeowner permission for identifiable properties.
